About Me

Who I Am

I am a Geospatial Data Scientist and PhD Candidate at the University of Central Florida, specializing in the intersection of climate intelligence and infrastructure resilience. My expertise lies in architecting automated geospatial pipelines to solve complex environmental challenges.

Before UCF, I earned a Master's in Geography from the University of Alabama, where I refined my skills in advanced spatial statistics and GIS-driven environmental modeling. Currently, as part of the DOE-funded CARES project, I leverage the ArcGIS API for Python, Google Earth Engine, and deep learning frameworks to model power grid vulnerabilities under compound weather extremes. My work transforms high-dimensional raster and vector data into actionable insights for grid hardening and community resilience.
