// 01 — About

Who I Am

I am a PhD candidate in the Department of Civil, Environmental, and Construction Engineering at the University of Central Florida. My research sits at the intersection of climate science, geospatial data analysis, and infrastructure resilience.

Before UCF, I completed my Master's in Geography from the University of Alabama, where I developed expertise in spatial statistics and environmental data analysis.

My current work focuses on big data analysis and machine/deep learning to model power outages in Florida caused by compound extreme weather events — with implications for vulnerable communities and grid resilience planning.
